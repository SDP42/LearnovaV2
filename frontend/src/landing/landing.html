Net present valueINVESTMENT APPRAISAL · 07
The formula
NPV = Σt=1nCFt(1 + r)t − I0
Accept if NPV > 0Reject if NPV < 0
CFtcash flow in year t
rdiscount rate, the cost of capital
I0initial investment today
Worked example: £10k now, £4k a year for 3 years at 8% → NPV = £3087 / 45

Investment Appraisal Criteria

1. Net Present Value (NPV) – NPV = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t} - I_0 the present value of future cash flows discounted at the cost of capital minus the initial investment, accept if NPV > 0 2. Internal Rate of Return (IRR) the rate which makes NPV equal to zero

3. Payback Period – number of years required to recover the initial outlay ignores time value of money 4. ARR = average annual profit / average investment 5. PI = PV of inflows / initial investment accept if PI > 1…

How k-means finds clustersCLUSTERING · 12
1 · Choose kSeed k centroids.
2 · AssignNearest centroid wins.
3 · UpdateCentroid = cluster mean.
4 · RepeatUntil nothing moves.
What it minimises
J = Σk=1KΣx∈Ck‖x − μk‖2
μk = centroid of cluster k

K-Means Clustering

• algorithm: first choose the number of clusters k and initialise k centroids randomly then assign each point to the nearest centroid using euclidean distance, objective J = \sum_{k=1}^{K} \sum_{x \in C_k} ||x - \mu_k||^2

• after assignment recompute each centroid as the mean of all points assigned to that cluster and repeat until the assignments no longer change, sensitive to initial centroids so k-means++ is often used, elbow method helps choose k…

Bayes' theorem, term by termBAYESIAN CLASSIFIER · 04
The rule
P(C | x) = P(x | C) · P(C)P(x)
Pick the class with the highest posterior
P(C | x)posterior: what we decide with
P(x | C)likelihood of the evidence
P(C)prior, before any data
Spam filter: P(spam | “free”) = 0.8 × 0.30.32 = 0.754 / 45

Bayesian Decision Theory

\( P(C|x) = \frac{P(x|C)P(C)}{P(x)} \) where P(C) is the prior probability of the class before observing the data and P(x|C) is the class likelihood which measures how probable the observation is given the class, P(x) is the evidence a normalising constant

example: spam filtering P(spam)=0.3 P(free|spam)=0.8 P(free)=0.32 so P(spam|free)=0.75 the posterior is used to choose the class with maximum a posteriori probability…
