## Photosynthesis Process
Photosynthesis happens in stages. First, chlorophyll absorbs sunlight in the thylakoid membranes. Next, water molecules are split to release oxygen and electrons. Then the energy is stored in ATP and NADPH. Finally, the Calvin cycle uses ATP and carbon dioxide to build glucose.

## History of Computing
In 1936 Alan Turing described the universal machine. In 1945 ENIAC became the first general purpose electronic computer. In 1971 Intel released the first microprocessor. In 1991 the World Wide Web opened to the public. In 2007 the iPhone launched the smartphone era.

## Supervised vs Unsupervised Learning
Supervised learning uses labelled data, while unsupervised learning finds patterns in unlabelled data. Supervised models predict outputs such as prices or classes. Unsupervised models group similar items, for example customers. Supervised learning needs costly labels, whereas unsupervised learning needs none but is harder to evaluate.

## Definition of Entropy
Entropy is a measure of the disorder or randomness in a system. In information theory, entropy quantifies the average uncertainty of a random variable. The formula H = -sum p(x) log p(x) gives the entropy of a distribution.

## Layers of the Earth
The Earth has four main layers. The crust is the thin outer shell. Beneath it lies the mantle, which is the thickest layer. The outer core is liquid iron and nickel. The inner core is solid and extremely hot.

## Newton's Second Law
The acceleration of an object depends on the net force acting on it and its mass. Force equals mass times acceleration. A heavier object needs more force to accelerate at the same rate.
