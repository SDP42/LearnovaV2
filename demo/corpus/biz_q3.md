# Quarterly Business Review

## Highlights
Revenue reached $12.4 million in Q3, up 18 percent from Q2. Gross margin improved from 41 percent to 46 percent. The company added 1,200 new customers and kept churn at 4 percent.

## Revenue by region
North America generated $5.6 million, Europe $3.9 million, Asia Pacific $2.1 million and the rest of the world $0.8 million. North America remains the largest region with 45 percent of revenue.

## Sales funnel
The team generated 8,000 leads. Of these, 3,200 became qualified prospects. 1,400 reached a demo, 520 received a proposal and 190 signed a contract.

## Product mix
Subscriptions provided 62 percent of revenue, services 24 percent and hardware 14 percent. Subscription revenue grew 27 percent year over year.

## Risks and actions
Supply delays could affect hardware shipments in Q4. Competitor pricing is putting pressure on mid-market deals. Therefore, the plan is to secure a second supplier, launch a loyalty discount and invest in customer success. The decision is needed by 15 November.

## Next quarter goals
Grow revenue to $14 million, improve gross margin to 48 percent and reduce churn to 3.5 percent.
