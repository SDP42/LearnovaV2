# The Water Cycle and Photosynthesis

## The water cycle
The water cycle moves water between the oceans, the air and the land. The sun heats surface water, which evaporates into vapour. The vapour rises, cools and condenses into clouds. Water falls as precipitation, flows over the land as runoff or soaks into the ground, and eventually returns to the sea, and the cycle begins again.

## Why the cycle matters
It supplies fresh water, shapes weather and distributes heat around the planet. Because water is constantly recycled, the total amount on Earth stays almost constant.

## What is photosynthesis
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. The overall equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2.

## Two stages
The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes and produce ATP, NADPH and oxygen. The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma and uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into sugar.

## Plants versus animals
Plants make their own food using light, so they are producers, whereas animals eat other organisms, so they are consumers. Plant cells have chloroplasts and cell walls; animal cells have neither.
