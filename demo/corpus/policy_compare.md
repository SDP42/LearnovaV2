# Remote Work Policy Review

## Background
After 2020 the company allowed remote work. Leadership now needs to decide on a long-term policy for 2,400 employees across 14 offices.

## Options
Option A is fully remote. Option B is hybrid with three office days per week. Option C is fully in office.

## Advantages of hybrid work
Hybrid work keeps collaboration time while saving commuting hours. It lets the company reduce office space by about 30 percent. Employees report higher satisfaction. However, scheduling is harder, and managers need training to lead mixed teams.

## Cost comparison
Fully remote costs $2.1 million a year in stipends and tools. Hybrid costs $3.4 million a year. Fully in office costs $5.9 million a year because of rent and utilities.

## Recommendation
We recommend the hybrid model. First, run a six month pilot in four offices. Second, measure productivity, retention and cost. Third, review the results with leadership in June and decide whether to extend it company wide.
