Region splitting and merging

Split and merge segments an image without seeds: the image is split into quadrants until every region is homogeneous, then adjacent regions are merged when their union is still homogeneous.

P(R) = TRUE ⇔ max(R) − min(R) ≤ T

Key ideas

  • Split: test P on a region; if it fails, divide it into four quadrants (a quadtree).
  • Merge: join two adjacent regions when P holds for their union.
  • A small T gives many regions; a large T merges real objects together.
  • Unlike region growing, no seed pixels are needed.
