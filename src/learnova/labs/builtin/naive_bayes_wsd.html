Naive Bayes word sense disambiguation

Word sense disambiguation picks the meaning of an ambiguous word (bass: fish or music?) from the words around it. Naive Bayes multiplies the sense's prior by the probability of each context word under that sense.

ŝ = argmax_s P(s) · Π P(w | s), P(w|s) = (count(w,s) + 1) / (|s| + V)

Key ideas

  • Prior P(s): how often the sense appears in the labelled data.
  • Likelihood P(w|s) with add-one smoothing so unseen words are not zero.
  • Multiply prior × likelihoods; the larger score wins.
  • Words never seen in training are ignored.
