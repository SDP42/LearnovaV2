Bigram probabilities, pair by pair

A bigram language model scores a sentence by multiplying the probability of each word given the word before it, estimated from counts in a corpus.

P(w1…wn) ≈ Π P(w_i | w_{i-1}), P(b|a) = count(a b) / count(a)

Key ideas

  • Pad sentences with <s> and </s>.
  • P(b|a) = count(a b) / count(a).
  • One unseen pair makes the whole sentence probability 0.
  • Add-one smoothing: (count + 1) / (count(a) + V).
